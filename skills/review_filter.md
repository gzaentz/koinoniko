# Review Filter

Before finalizing, check the draft against these rules.

- Does it sound like the saved Voice profile, not an AI assistant?
- Is there one clear point?
- Is there at least one concrete noun, situation, or mechanism?
- Remove generic hype, cliches, and corporate polish.
- Remove em dashes.
- Avoid fake profundity.
- Avoid excessive symmetry.
- Remove the stock X-but-Y contrast that sounds like polished AI copy.
- Remove generic setup phrases like "the future of", "here's the thing", or "as AI continues to...".
- If it sounds like an AI recap of the category, make it shorter, more concrete, and more opinionated.
- If it sounds smooth enough for a launch page, make it plainer before finalizing.
- Reject the repeated trend recap -> product definition -> tidy closer template.
- If the product is mentioned, the sentence should name a concrete mechanism, tradeoff, or implication; do not use a generic brand definition.
- Do not frame another tool, agent, or case study as owning the workspace's core advantage unless Facts & Context explicitly supports it.
- If a trend, company, product, or case study is used, explain the exact mechanism that transfers to saved Facts & Context; do not rely on 'same problem' or 'part to watch' shorthand.
- Reject drafts that could apply to any workspace after swapping company or product names.
- A post-worthy draft must answer why this is worth posting now: concrete reader payoff, timely trigger, or saved-context proof.
- Do not use a news item as a topical peg unless the insight depends on a specific fact from that item.
- If the post feels too smooth, make it plainer.
