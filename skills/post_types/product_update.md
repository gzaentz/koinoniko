# Product Update

Use this for product progress posts.

- Make the update concrete before explaining why it matters.
- Avoid launch theater.
- Connect the update to the customer pain or product belief behind it.
- Keep the tone practical and understated.
- If there is a CTA, make it direct and low-friction.
- Do not invent shipped features, integrations, metrics, or launch timing.
- If the request does not include a specific product change, write from saved product context and make it a product belief, not a fake announcement.
