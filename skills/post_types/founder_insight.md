# Founder Insight

Use this for founder-led point-of-view posts.

- Start from something the writer has noticed, learned, or now believes.
- Name the broken default when useful.
- Give the sharper model in plain English.
- Keep it grounded in product, customer, market, or building reality.
- Add one reader payoff beyond the opinion: a mechanism, diagnostic, decision rule, practical step, tradeoff, or evidence boundary.
- Rotate the kind of insight. Do not make every founder post a broken-default teardown or route every topic back to the same positioning wedge.
- End without a CTA unless a conversation invite feels natural.
